Terms of use

Personal use aboard Neverland.

Neverland Calendar Sync is a private scheduling connection operated by the boat's system owner. It is not a public subscription service.

Authorized use

Access to the boat's calendars is managed separately by their owner. Visiting this information website does not grant calendar access. Authorized calendar users should enter information appropriate for the people with whom those calendars are shared.

Availability and conflicts

Synchronization depends on the NAS, Google authorization, and an available internet connection. Offline changes may remain local until the connection returns. Conflicting edits may require administrator review. Calendar synchronization does not guarantee immediate delivery of reminders or changes.

Operational decisions

The calendar is a scheduling aid. Crew should confirm watch assignments, urgent maintenance, and safety-critical instructions directly with the person responsible rather than rely solely on a calendar notification.

Privacy and access changes

Information is handled as described in the privacy notice. The owner can stop synchronization or change calendar sharing. Existing events and recovery copies require separate deletion when no longer needed.

Contact

Contact the project owner through the project support page. Do not post private calendar information or credentials in a public issue.
