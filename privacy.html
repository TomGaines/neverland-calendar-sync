How calendar information is used.

This personal connection exists to keep Neverland's two boat calendars available on the boat and in the owner's Google account.

Information accessed

The connection reads and synchronizes events in Neverland Crew and Neverland Maintenance. Events may contain titles, descriptions, dates, times, locations, recurrence rules, and participant information supplied by calendar users.

Google authorization permits broader Calendar access. The synchronization configuration restricts operation to these two selected calendars. Google calendar discovery may read calendar names and identifiers to find the selected calendars.

Use and sharing

Calendar information is used to synchronize these calendars and display the selected boat schedule in Neverland's local calendar preview. Selected events and edits are exchanged between the owner's Synology NAS and Google Calendar. Calendar content is not published on this information website, sold, or used for advertising.

Storage and recovery copies

Operational data and Google authorization tokens are stored privately on the owner's Synology NAS. The setup process may temporarily keep protected authorization files on the owner's setup computer. Google retains the synchronized calendars under the owner's Google account.

The NAS retains an initial recovery snapshot until the owner removes it, plus approximately one day of snapshots made before synchronization. Offline access and recovery therefore involve local copies of selected calendar information.

Disconnecting and deleting information

The owner can revoke the connection in Google Account third-party connections and stop the NAS synchronization container. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete existing events or local recovery copies. The owner can separately remove events and recovery files from Google Calendar and Synology.

Website hosting

These static information pages do not use analytics, advertising, account forms, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts these information pages. GitHub may process ordinary connection information under its privacy statement.

Contact

Contact the project owner through the project support page. Do not post private calendar information or credentials in a public issue.
