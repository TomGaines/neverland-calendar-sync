Private boat calendar connection

A schedule that stays aboard.

Neverland Calendar Sync connects two boat calendars on an onboard Synology NAS with the owner's Google Calendar account.

Two calendars, one shared schedule

  • Neverland Crew: boat activities and crew scheduling.
  • Neverland Maintenance: scheduled maintenance and reminders.

When an internet connection is available, the connection synchronizes calendar events in both directions about every five minutes. While offshore, the local Synology calendars remain available on the boat's network. Changes can synchronize after connectivity returns.

This is a personal Neverland project. This website provides information about the connection. It does not provide public account registration, display calendar events, or connect visitors to the boat's systems.

Google access

The connector requests Google Calendar permission that is broader than these two calendars. Its configuration selects only Neverland Crew and Neverland Maintenance for synchronization. Personal calendars are excluded from that configuration.

Read the privacy notice for details on access, storage, and disconnecting the service.
